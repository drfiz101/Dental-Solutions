Getting started

Everyone does this once on their own phone.

Sign in

  1. Open the app link your clinic sent you on WhatsApp.
  2. Enter the Clinic code (for example alawan). If you used your clinic's link, it is already filled in.
  3. Enter your Username and Password, then tap Sign in.
The phone remembers your clinic code and username, so next time you only type your password.

Install it like an app

  • iPhone (Safari): tap the Share button ⬆ → Add to Home Screen.
  • Android (Chrome): tap ⋮ → Install app or Add to Home screen.
Once installed, it opens full-screen from your home screen and works even when the internet is slow.

Change your password

Open Menu → Change password. Do this after your first sign-in if someone else set your password.

For the clinic owner

Set up your clinic once. This takes about 15 minutes.

Register your clinic

  1. On the sign-in screen, choose Register a clinic.
  2. Fill in the clinic name, a short clinic code (letters and numbers only), city, phone, your name, a username and a password.
  3. Tap Register clinic. Your clinic waits for approval by Dental Solutions, and then opens by itself.
If Dental Solutions created the clinic for you, skip this. Just sign in with the details they sent on WhatsApp.

First-day setup checklist

  1. Clinic profile: Menu → Clinic profile & WhatsApp messages. Add your logo, phone, tagline, opening days and hours, and appointment length. Bills and messages use these details.
  2. Dentists: in Appointments → Team, add your dentists so you can choose them when booking.
  3. Treatment prices: Menu → Treatment prices. Enter your price for each procedure. Bills and treatment plans fill these in automatically.
  4. Labs: in Lab → Labs, add the labs you use, with their WhatsApp number and usual turnaround days.
  5. Stock: in the Inventory tab, add the materials you keep and the minimum level for each one.
  6. Staff: add your team (see Admin below).

For admins

Admins can see everything and manage the team. The clinic owner is always an admin.

Add a staff member

  1. Open Menu → Staff access → + Add.
  2. Type their name, a username (for example bilal) and a starting password.
  3. Choose a role: Admin (full access) or Staff.
  4. For staff, tap a quick set (Receptionist, Dental assistant or Dentist), then tick or untick anything you want to change.
  5. Tap Save. Give them the clinic code, their username and password.
Quick setWhat they can do

Manage the team

  • Edit access: change ticks on their card and tap Save.
  • Forgotten password: tap 🔑 Set new password on their card. Their username stays the same.
  • Someone leaves: untick Login allowed, or delete the login. Their past work stays in the records.
Money figures (income and profit) are only visible to staff who have the "See income & profit" tick.

Daily work, step by step

The tasks you will do most often.

Register a new patient

  1. Tap New patient on the dashboard, or + in Patients.
  2. Enter the name and WhatsApp number. Add age, gender and any medical alerts (for example Diabetic, On blood thinners).
  3. Tap Save. A file number is given automatically.
If the patient is already registered, the app tells you and offers to open their file. If the number belongs to someone else (for example a family member), it asks before adding the same number again.

Book an appointment

  1. Tap Book, or tap a free time under Chair 1 or Chair 2.
  2. Type the patient's name or phone and pick them, or tap + New patient.
  3. Choose the date, time, chair, dentist and treatment. Busy times are blocked automatically.
  4. Tap Save, then send the confirmation on WhatsApp.
  • Change the time: open the appointment → Reschedule, pick the new date and time.
  • Status: mark the patient as arrived, done or no-show as the day goes on.
  • Reminders: the day before, find WhatsApp reminders for tomorrow on the dashboard and tap Send reminder for each patient.

Treatment plan

  1. Open the patient → Treatment plan → add procedures. Prices fill in from your price list, and you can tap teeth on the chart.
  2. Add a discount if needed and share the plan as a PDF proposal on WhatsApp.
  3. When a procedure is finished, tick Done.
  4. When it is paid, tick Paid. The app asks how it was paid and makes the bill for you.
Done and Paid are separate. For example, a denture can be paid in advance but not fitted yet.

Bills and payments

  1. Tap New bill, choose the patient and add treatments. Prices fill in automatically.
  2. If they pay now, enter the amount and how they paid: Cash, Bank transfer, Easypaisa, JazzCash, NayaPay, SadaPay or Card. For bank transfers, pick the bank (or + Add other bank).
  3. Tap Save, then WhatsApp or PDF to send the invoice.
  • Part payment later: open the bill → enter the amount → Add payment.
  • Paid in full: tick Paid in full on the bill.
  • Wrong payment method or amount: tap ✎ Edit next to that payment.
  • Who owes money: Dashboard → Patients owe.

Lab work

  1. In Lab, tap + New lab case. Choose the patient, lab, work type, teeth and shade.
  2. The due date is set from the lab's turnaround. Send the case details to the lab on WhatsApp.
  3. Update the status as it moves: At lab → Trial → Back from lab → Fitted.
Late cases are highlighted, and you get a sound alert on the day a case is due back.

Stock

  • Tap − or + on an item when you use or receive it.
  • Items below their minimum level show as low. Use the list to reorder.
  • Record purchases and vendor bills so spending shows in Reports.

Reports (admins)

Choose a date range to see income, payments by method and bank, billing by dentist, lab costs, expenses and profit. Tap PDF to save or share the report.

Common questions

I forgot my password
Ask your clinic admin to tap 🔑 Set new password on your card in Staff access. If you are the owner, contact Dental Solutions on WhatsApp.
I can't see a tab or button
Your admin has not given you that permission. Ask them to tick it in Staff access.
The app looks old or a new feature is missing
Close the app completely and open it again. It updates itself when it opens.
The app won't open
If you see a Repair app button, tap it. It clears the old copy and loads a fresh one. Your clinic data is safe because it is stored online.
Is our patient data private?
Yes. Each clinic only sees its own patients and money. Staff only see what their admin allows. Not even Dental Solutions can open your patients or bills.
Can two people use it at the same time?
Yes. Reception, dentists and assistants can all use it on their own phones together. Changes appear on everyone's screen within seconds.
